
                             Credits and Thanks!


I considered making a detailed list of all the things that various members 
worked on. But since we are a team and most wear several hats, I won't. 
Instead, at the bottom is a list of everyone that contributed to the making of
HACX. There is also a credits screen in the game, listing the main people 
that made HACX and what they did. Watch the demo, just start the game and 
sit back.

I want to thank the many people that have supported us, family members and
friends, that have invested their time and support, allowing all of us to 
spend many hours in front of tiny CRT's!

To those that are members of Banjo, you have made me look very good, and 
I'm biting at the bit to start on our next adventure! Your the best group 
of people I've ever worked with!!

To those that are reading this and are merely curious or, interested in 
playing our game, we thank you, because as much as anything, the applause
is the wine of creation!
